Common Questions

Everything you're wondering

How much does a remodel cost?

Every project is different, which is why we provide a free, detailed written estimate based on your exact scope. Request one above or call 215-554-5488 — there's never any obligation.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Bensalem and all of Bucks County plus Greater Philadelphia — including Levittown, Feasterville, Newtown, Langhorne and Yardley.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — All Home Building Co. is a fully licensed Pennsylvania contractor carrying liability insurance, and every project is backed by our workmanship warranty.

How long will my project take?

Your written estimate includes a realistic timeline, and we provide progress updates throughout. Bathrooms often take 2–3 weeks; kitchens and additions vary by scope.

Do you offer financing?

Ask your specialist — we can walk you through payment and financing options for qualifying projects.

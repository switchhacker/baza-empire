Common Questions

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Philadelphia?

Kitchen remodel costs in Philadelphia typically range from $15,000 to $75,000 depending on scope, materials, and finishes. Contact us for a free estimate specific to your project.

Do I need a permit for home renovation in Philadelphia?

Most significant renovations in Philadelphia require permits from L&I (Licenses & Inspections). We handle all permit applications and inspections as part of our project management.

How long does a typical home renovation take?

Timeline varies by project: bathroom renovations take 2-4 weeks, kitchen remodels 4-8 weeks, additions 8-16 weeks, and full rehabs 3-6 months. We provide detailed timelines in every estimate.
